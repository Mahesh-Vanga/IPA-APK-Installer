# Changelog

## 0.0.3

- Return the menu bar installer to a fresh process after the panel is closed and the app becomes idle. Retain the selected build, device, and diagnostics locally.
- Preserve menu bar drag-and-drop, detachable panel, and manual update checks.
- Publish buildable source, offline tests, reproducible build instructions, and contributor and privacy documentation.

## 0.0.1

- First public release of the native menu bar installer for signed IPA and APK files.
- Apple silicon and Intel downloads, direct Finder drops, and optional launch at login.
